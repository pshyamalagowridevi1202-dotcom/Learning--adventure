MAGIC SKY RUNNER

Dash through the clouds, dodge sparkling obstacles, catch glowing stars, and guide your little explorer with just your index finger.

  • Move your index finger left or right to glide
  • Lift your finger upward to jump
  • Collect glowing stars and avoid magical blockers
  • Play with camera controls only
Magic • Catch • Smile
✨

Star Catcher

Catch magical stars using your index finger and build a glowing sky-high score!

  • Move your index finger to catch stars
  • Golden stars give bonus points
  • Beat the 60-second challenge
  • Play with camera controls only
Magic • Drag • Sparkle
🪄

Magic Drag

Drag magical objects using your index finger and match them to the glowing magical zones!

  • Move your index finger over an object
  • Lift and drag it to the matching magical zone
  • Match stars, moons, clouds, gems, and more
  • Play with camera controls only
Build • Balance • Glow
🧱

Block Builder

Guide magical blocks with your index finger, drop them carefully, and build the tallest glowing tower you can.

  • Move your index finger to position each block
  • Hold steady to drop the block safely
  • Stack with perfect balance for bonus points
  • Play with camera controls only
Focus • Aim • Magic
🎯

MAGIC TARGET HUNT

Follow the glowing fingertip, lock onto each magical target, and build a sparkling combo before time runs out.

  • Use only your index fingertip
  • Move to the glowing target and tap it
  • Earn combo points and golden bonuses
  • Beat the timer before the portal closes
Dodge • Move • Survive
⚡

NEON DODGE

Guide your glowing explorer through a magical neon world, dodge moving obstacles, collect stars, and survive the challenge using your index finger.

  • Move your index finger to guide the explorer
  • Dodge the glowing obstacles
  • Collect stars to increase your score
  • Survive the challenge and beat your best score
